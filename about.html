About us

A home kitchenwith a gift-wrapper's heart.

Curated by Mona is a small, women-owned home business in Coimbatore — part kitchen, part gifting studio.

“She puts her heart out in every hamper she makes.”
Our story

Every order is made for one person.

Curated by Mona is for the moments people want to get just right: a platter for a birthday at home, food for a weekend tournament, a hamper for someone far away, or a trousseau that arrives looking as special as it feels.

Nothing comes off a shelf. Mona listens to the occasion and the person, then shapes the menu, the hamper or the décor around them — the flavours, the colours, the little finishing touches.

Customers tell us they notice it most in the presentation, and in the care that goes into each piece. That's the part we never rush.
